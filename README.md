# Project "ShroomFit"

## Description of the solution
Have you ever tried following a new routine, building a new habit? Just how often were you failing to keep up the pace? For a teen or adult, in a new, modernised world, it's gotten increasingly more difficult to build reliable habits that directly affect your health and well-being. That's where ShroomFit comes in clutch.

The Web Page (and the mobile app[in progress]) will help you keep consistent with activities aimed to maintain good health daily, for not more than 15-30 minutes depending on your activity, with a gamified structure and concrete workout routine made for you after the initial poll to identify your needs.

"ShroomFit" will have you accompanied with in-game mushrooms! You will grow more mushrooms as you complete more workouts, go to app's recommended gyms that are fit best for your situation and keep your streak on fire. You can also purchase additional "skins" for your adorable companions, bringing them a refreshing appeal.
